# Security Policy

Please **do not open a public issue** for a security problem (for example a way around plans, limits or payments,
or a leaked key).

Report it privately through GitHub: Security tab -> "Report a vulnerability" on this repository.
Include steps to reproduce. We aim to answer within 5 days and to credit you if you wish.

Out of scope: running a modified copy on your own servers with your own limits (that is allowed by the license).
In scope: anything that lets a user use the official hosted service beyond their paid plan, read another user's data,
or obtain our keys.
